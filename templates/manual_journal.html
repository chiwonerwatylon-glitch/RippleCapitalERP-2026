💡 Accounting Guidelines

Double-entry principle: every journal entry should have both a debit and a credit account with the same amount.

Example – Recording a $1,000 commission expense:

  • Debit: Commission Expense
  • Credit: Commission Payable
  • Amount: $1,000.00

Use manual journal entries for adjustments, corrections, or one-off accounting events that are not captured automatically.
